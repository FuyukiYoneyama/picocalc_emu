## Decision

The unreachable backend pin used by the historical VRP-2-f records was not
reused.  A new versioned launch target, `picotetris-opt1b-vrp5` revision 10,
was created and validated against reachable backend commit
`65c795e87321e79b960ac8a7495a205de6a24ec0` from `main`.

The old `picotetris-opt1b-vrp2f` revision 8 and its evidence remain
unchanged.  The repository backend worktree's pre-existing uncommitted
changes were not used; the recorded run used the clean temporary checkout
`/tmp/vrp-load0-backend-clean-r2`.

## What this does and does not establish

This closes the reusable backend-pin preflight: a reachable backend, clean
runner, reproducible artifact pair, versioned target/validation, receipt,
admission, and headless preview consumer are connected by the recorded
identity and hashes.

It does not claim any of the following:

- completion of the repository-owned `VRP-LOAD-0` vertical slice or its
  three-run preparation gate;
- a frozen realtime threshold or a `REALTIME OK` result;
- `realtime-1x-qualified` or a measured input-to-visible-response UX result;
- hardware correlation; or
- any dependency on, branch of, publication of, or modification to
  `Picocalc_NESco`.

The current `VRP-LOAD-0` prototype run remains a separate in-progress task.
Its formal target registration and VRP-5 qualification remain gated by the
plan's completion and threshold-decision requirements.
